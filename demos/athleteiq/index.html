What it answers

Questions a coaching staff asks before training

Each answer comes from the player records loaded in the bot. In this mock-up those records belong to a fictional squad.

01
Who to watch for injury
Crosses injury history, training load, session quality and mental state, and names the players to watch with the reason.
02
Stats per player
Run metres, tackle efficiency, post-contact metres and try assists, per game and per 80 minutes.
03
Voice notes and photos
From the pitch you can send a voice note or a photo instead of typing. The bot transcribes it and answers in the chat.
04
Players with little game time
Looks for players with high output per 80 minutes who have played few minutes.
05
Rugby league vocabulary
Uses the sport’s terms and positions, and treats pre-season questions apart from in-season ones.
06
Answers in your language
It replies in the language you wrote in. There is no dashboard to learn.

How it works

What happens when you ask

  1. 1
    You write, or send a voice note
    The question goes into a regular chat. There is no app to install.
  2. 2
    The bot reads the records
    It looks up the players involved: injuries with dates, training scores, mental state and last season’s stats.
  3. 3
    It answers with a recommendation
    A short reply with the numbers it used and what it would do, in the language of the question.
  4. 4
    What is missing
    It has not been tested with a real club, and no medical team has checked its answers.
The project

Designed around one squad

I designed AthleteIQ as a pitch for a professional rugby league club. In this copy the club and its players are fictional.

Each fictional player has an injury history with dates, scores from recent training sessions, a note on mental state and stats from the previous club.

Status: prototype. The landing, the bots and the fictional squad exist; there are no clients or contracts.
