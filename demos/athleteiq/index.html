The Project

Built for one club.
From day one.

AthleteIQ was designed as a demo for a professional rugby league club's first season. The demo squad holds 19 fictional players, each with a complete record.

Every player record includes injury history with exact dates, training quality scores from the last 5 sessions, psychological state and statistics from their most recent club.

This is not generic sports analytics software. It is built around one squad, one season, one coaching staff.
